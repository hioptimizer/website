    Before you run anything as Administrator

    Is this safe to run on a laptop?

    Most modules are fine. The Power Tweaks module specifically disables sleep, hibernation, and fast startup, and is flagged "not recommended for laptops" in the tool itself — skip that one on battery-powered devices.

    What's the actual difference between Free and Premium?

    Free gives you 10 modules: CPU, GPU, Debloat, Services, Network, RAM, Latency Tweaks, System Tweaks, Wifi Fix, and Windows Cleanup & Repair. Most of those run a basic tweak set in Free and the full set in Premium — that includes Debloat, where Premium clears out OEM bloatware and forced-install apps the free pass leaves behind. Premium also adds six modules Free doesn't have at all: Mouse Tweaks, Keyboard Tweaks, Extra Windows Optimization, Disk Tweaks, Game Booster, and Power Tweaks. If you want your PC to feel genuinely different, Premium is the one to get, and it's a one-time $5 with lifetime updates and support included.

    What is "Extra Windows Optimization", exactly?

    It's the biggest single module in Premium — roughly 1,000 lines of additional tweaks layered on top of the standard modules, covering registry, Group Policy, scheduler, and power settings the other modules don't touch. It's still plain-text and reversible, it's just a lot more of it in one place.

    Can I undo everything afterward?

    Yes — restore the System Restore checkpoint from launch, or re-import the .reg backups from C:\RegistryBackup. Any "Disable" module also has a matching "Re-enable" entry in the same menu.

    Does it need an internet connection?

    No — every module runs local registry, service, and power-plan commands. Nothing is downloaded or phoned home.

    Why did the site's design change?

    The old "liquid glass" look leaned hard on blur effects that either didn't render at all on weak or GPU-less devices, or made the page choppy to scroll on older hardware. This version keeps the same terminal look but with a solid background under every panel, so it reads fine with or without blur, uses far less animation, and should scroll smoothly even on a single-core CPU. You can also change the accent color from the dots in the header — whichever one you pick is used everywhere on the page, not just on a couple of buttons.
